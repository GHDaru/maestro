# Prompts de imagem — deck executivo Maestro (1 por slide)

Para gerar em Midjourney / DALL·E / Firefly etc. Estilo **consistente** entre slides
(mesma direção de arte). Imagens são **fundo/apoio** — o texto é sobreposto no deck,
então os prompts pedem **sem texto** e com **espaço negativo**.

**Sufixo de estilo (cole no fim de todo prompt):**
> cinematic editorial concept art, deep indigo-ink background, warm brass/gold light,
> volumetric haze, premium and minimal, lots of negative space, subtle orchestral/score
> motifs, no text, no logos, 16:9 --style raw

---

## 01 · Abertura — "Maestro"
A lone conductor seen from behind, baton raised, facing a vast dark concert hall where the
orchestra is made of faint glowing geometric nodes and threads of light instead of
musicians; brass rim light on the silhouette; cinematic wide shot. + sufixo de estilo.

## 02 · O que é
A single human hand holding a conductor's baton that emits fine golden light-threads
branching out to many small luminous cubes arranged like an orchestra; elegant, macro
foreground to wide background. + sufixo de estilo.

## 03 · A dor (por que surgiu)
Diptych concept: left half a crowded dim archive of tangled cables, stacked paper and grey
faceless figures buried in old code (cold slate, heavy); right half a chaotic burst of
ungoverned light with no conductor; between them a dark gap. + sufixo de estilo.

## 04 · Mapa da metodologia
An elegant exploded diagram floating in dark space: five horizontal luminous strata stacked
like a musical staff, each layer holding small glowing tokens connected by thin brass lines;
blueprint-meets-score, isometric, orderly. + sufixo de estilo.

## 05 · Fundamentação teórica
Several marble-and-obsidian plinths standing in an indigo void, each engraved with subtle
abstract glyphs blending classical and modern motifs, grazed by warm brass light; solid,
authoritative, foundational. + sufixo de estilo.

## 06 · Os seis pilares
Six slender vertical columns of brass light rising from a dark reflective floor like organ
pipes, each capped with a different subtle glyph, arranged in a rhythmic symmetrical row. +
sufixo de estilo.

## 07 · Antes × Depois da IA
Clean diptych: left panel a heavy grey machine hall with dozens of tiny identical grey
figures at desks (slow, dense, cold); right panel one warm-lit conductor guiding streams of
golden light doing the same work; stark side-by-side contrast. + sufixo de estilo.

## 08 · ROI em legado
An ancient tangled monolith of dark stone and old circuitry (a legacy system) being
illuminated and reorganized by threads of warm brass light that map its structure into a
clean glowing lattice; archaeology meets light. + sufixo de estilo.

## 09 · Prova viva
An open manuscript / score that writes and rewrites itself, pages turning as glowing brass
annotations appear in the margins, arrows of light forming a feedback loop around it;
self-generating, alive. + sufixo de estilo.

## 10 · Próximo movimento (fecho)
A conductor's baton resting on an open score at the edge of a dark stage, a single soft
spotlight and the silent hall waiting in anticipation; warm brass highlight, vast negative
space. + sufixo de estilo.

---

### Notas de uso
- **Coerência**: gere todas com o mesmo modelo/seed-family e o mesmo sufixo de estilo.
- **Paleta**: índigo-tinta (#0d1023) + latão (#e6c375). Peça "gold/brass, indigo, no
  other bright colors" se o gerador desviar.
- **Sem texto**: o deck já tem a tipografia; imagens com texto competem e poluem.
- **Formato**: 16:9 para casar com os slides; deixe respiro para o texto sobreposto
  (composição com o assunto deslocado para um lado).
